## What is AI-native transcription

Acoustic speech models hear local sound frames. Lanson Live adds a dedicated reasoning model inside the live transcription loop: acoustic recognition is the first signal, and a separate reasoning model with full conversation context reviews every word before any utterance is delivered.

Concretely, speech is held briefly inside a StableStream™ segment boundary instead of being emitted as flickering word-by-word drafts. That gives the reasoning layer the time and global context to verify each utterance—domain terms, proper nouns, references from earlier turns—before the application receives it as final.

## What is the cadence and buffer trade-off

Real-time is a delivery strategy matched to the application. Waiting for finalization introduces a deliberate trade-off: a longer segment gives the reasoning model more surrounding speech to work with, while a shorter segment delivers updates faster.

## What is the delivery boundary

Take a conversation about integrating the Lanson Live API. The **delivery boundary** is the architectural detail: both acoustic recognition and independent reasoning review finish inside the pipeline, so the application receives the verified transcript as its first and only event.
