**LiveFinal™** defines the finalization boundary in Lanson Live. It integrates candidate speech recognition and context-aware refinement behind a single delivery boundary—so your application receives one reviewed, completed transcript event per utterance rather than a stream of provisional drafts and retroactive patches.

::ai-native-pillar-visual{pillar="livefinal"}
::

## Why the delivery boundary defines the developer experience

When you build on a conventional streaming STT API—or bolt an external LLM onto an STT stream—the **delivery boundary is in the wrong place**:

- **Too early (Traditional STT)**: Unreviewed acoustic guesses cross the boundary as `partial` events, and `is_final` only signals that the acoustic decoder stopped updating its buffer—with zero semantic review.
- **Split across two services (DIY STT + LLM)**: Raw STT crosses the boundary first, and an external LLM patch arrives later—forcing your UI to either stall or visually rewrite text the user has already read.

LiveFinal™ places the delivery boundary **after** in-pipeline context review:

```text
[ Audio Stream ] ──► [ Candidate Recognition ] ──► [ Context-Aware Review ] ║ ──► [ Completed Event ]
                     └────────── Internal Pipeline (Not Emitted) ─────────┘ ║     (Delivered to App)
                                                                      Delivery Boundary
```

::ai-native-delivery-boundary
::

## The single-handoff contract

In the realtime WebSocket path (`WS /v1/audio/transcriptions/stream`), each successfully processed utterance produces **one** `conversation.item.input_audio_transcription.completed` event:

- **`text`**: The finalized transcript after in-pipeline review.
- **`raw_text`**: The initial acoustic recognition output prior to refinement, preserved for auditability.
- **`transcript.state`**: Delivered as `"stable"` (`revision: 0`)—this endpoint will never emit a later revision event that mutates an already-delivered utterance.
- **`transcript.correction_applied`**: Boolean flag indicating whether in-pipeline refinement changed `raw_text`.

## What "completed" and "stable" mean

Clarity of contract is essential for reliable systems:

- **A delivery boundary guarantee**: `completed` and `stable` guarantee that the pipeline has finished recognition and contextual review for that segment and will not retroactively overwrite it.
- **Not an infallibility claim**: Real-time speech can still contain acoustic ambiguity or domain terms outside the session's context. Always use `utterance_index` for ordering and inspect `transcript.safe_for_downstream` before triggering automated downstream actions.
