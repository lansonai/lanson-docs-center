**LiveFinal™** integrates reasoning review directly into utterance finalization. A candidate transcript is evaluated before the completed event is delivered, rather than asking your application to display one version and patch it after another service finishes correcting it.

In the realtime path, the handoff is one completed result per successfully processed utterance, with transcript state and review metadata. Completed means the pipeline has finished processing that utterance; it does not promise that every word is error-free.
