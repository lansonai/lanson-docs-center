**StableStream™** is the foundation of Lanson Live's approach. Instead of exposing continuously changing word-by-word guesses, the realtime transcription path delivers stable, completed utterances.

That choice matters for two reasons. Readers are not forced to follow text that constantly rewrites itself, and developers receive a clearer delivery contract without building UI reconciliation for repeated partial revisions.

Most importantly, finalization creates the window needed for a separate reasoning model to review the acoustic candidate before delivery. The segment boundary determines how much speech can be evaluated together.

## What is segment duration

Once speech is held inside a bounded segment window so reasoning review can run before delivery, **segment duration** is the lever for matching the pipeline to the scenario:

| Scenario Profile | Typical Window | What the Window Provides |
|---|---|---|
| **Voice Interfaces & Turn-Taking** | `2–4s` | Delivers frequent completed utterances for interactive turn-taking. |
| **Live Conversations & Meetings** | `5–10s` | Gives full clause and sentence boundaries, balancing responsive captions with rich intra-segment context. |
| **Broadcasts & Caption Feeds** | `12s+` | Buffers multi-clause passages when timing is flexible, maximizing surrounding speech context. |
