# Data Retention

## Realtime sessions

- Audio is streamed through the gateway and **not persisted**. There is no server-side recording of a live session.
- Transcript segments are delivered to your client over the WebSocket; keeping them is your responsibility. See [Save a Transcript](/docs/guides/save-transcript).

## Recorded / batch transcription

- Batch jobs fetch audio from the `audio_url` you provide. Long jobs checkpoint intermediate results in object storage so a re-submitted `request_id` can resume ([Batch Jobs](/docs/api-reference/batch-jobs)).
- The final transcript is returned in the job response and/or posted to your `webhook_url`.

## Deletion requests

- To request deletion of any retained data associated with your account, contact `support@lansonai.com`.
